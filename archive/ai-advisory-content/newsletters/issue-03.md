**MY WEEK IN AI: REAL TIME SAVINGS**

**Monday — Investor Pipeline Work**
I needed to update 11 LP contact records and draft personalized follow-up notes for each one based on our last interaction. Old way: 45 minutes minimum. With AI: I fed Claude a template, my CRM notes, and asked it to draft 11 individual follow-ups with different openers and specific callbacks to our last conversation. Time: 14 minutes. **Time saved: ~31 minutes.**

**Tuesday — Client Strategy Memo**
A mid-market client needed a memo summarizing three months of advisory work and our recommended pivot. Normally a 3-hour writing project minimum. I fed Claude my bullet-point notes, a prior email thread, and a framework I use for strategy pivots. First draft in 11 minutes. Editing and personalizing: 22 minutes. Total: 33 minutes. **Time saved: ~2.5 hours.**

**Wednesday — Market Research Brief**
Used Perplexity to pull current data on the Antelope Valley commercial real estate market for a deal I'm evaluating. Live citations, current data, organized by the categories I specified. Would have taken 2+ hours manually. Time with AI: 18 minutes. **Time saved: ~1.5–2 hours.**

**Thursday — Content Repurposing**
Took one newsletter (last week's issue) and ran it through the Content Repurpose Prompt. Got a LinkedIn post, three Instagram captions, a Twitter thread, and a slide pull-quote — all in 8 minutes. **Time saved: ~1.5 hours.**

**Friday — Proposal Follow-Up**
Used the 8-minute proposal workflow from last week. Two proposals. 16 minutes total. **Time saved: ~4 hours.**

---

**THE HONEST TOTAL**

Hours saved across the week: **~12 hours.**

Not every task was perfect on the first pass. Two of the investor follow-ups needed significant rewrites because my CRM notes were too thin. Garbage in, garbage out — that principle still applies.

But 12 hours. At my advisory rate, that's a significant amount of recaptured billable capacity. Or just — my kids' soccer games, without the guilt of what I'm not getting done.

**WHAT AI STILL CAN'T DO FOR YOU**

- It cannot read the room on a call.
- It cannot build a relationship.
- It cannot make the judgment call on whether to push a deal forward or walk away.
- It cannot replace your reputation.

The leaders winning with AI are not trying to replace these things. They're using AI to clear the operational noise so they can do more of the things only they can do.

That's the whole game.

**ALSO THIS WEEK: THE SAFETY REMINDER**

Quick note because it came up with a client: **do not paste real client names + deal values together into any free-tier AI tool.**

Use "Client A" and general ranges. You still get 95% of the output quality. And you protect your clients' confidentiality, which protects your reputation.

More on AI safety rules next week — I'm doing a full breakdown.
