**THE 8-MINUTE PROPOSAL WORKFLOW**

This works because I've stopped treating AI like a magic wand and started treating it like a smart first-year analyst. I give it structure, context, and constraints. It gives me a professional first draft. I review and refine in under 10 minutes.

**Step 1: Capture during the call (2 min)**
While I'm on the call, I jot raw notes into a Notes app. Not organized. Not formatted. Just everything that matters: the problem they described, what they've already tried, what success looks like to them, their timeline, any numbers they mentioned. That's it.

**Step 2: Drop notes into Claude with this prompt (1 min)**

> *"You are my strategic business advisor. I just finished a client discovery call. Turn the following raw notes into a structured one-page executive proposal with four sections: (1) The Challenge, (2) The Approach, (3) The Investment, (4) The Timeline. Use concise, executive-level language. No filler. No jargon. Write it as if the client's CEO will read it."*

Then paste your notes. All of them, messy as they are.

**Step 3: Add your brand voice (1 min)**

Before you hit send, add this second prompt:

> *"Now rewrite this in my voice: bold, empathetic, direct, and action-oriented. I'm a woman who runs a PE advisory firm. Remove any hedging. Replace passive verbs with active ones. Make it feel like a peer talking to a peer."*

**Step 4: Review + personalize the numbers (3 min)**
AI cannot know your pricing. Review every number and make sure it reflects your actual scope. Add one or two specific details from the call that show you were genuinely listening. That's the human layer. That's the part that closes the deal.

**Step 5: Send (1 min)**
Copy into your branded template or email. Send. Done.

---

**WHAT THIS IS NOT**

This is not a way to skip thinking.

The quality of your output depends entirely on the quality of your call notes. If you're a lazy listener, AI won't save you. If you're sharp in discovery, AI will make your sharpness visible in record time.
