**THE FOUR PLATFORMS YOU NEED TO KNOW**

**Claude — Your Strategist**
This is the one I use most for high-stakes business writing. Investor memos. Brand voice documents. Long-form strategy. Client proposals. Anything where nuance, tone, and accuracy matter. Claude has the lowest hallucination rate of any major model and it doesn't try to sound smart — it just *is* smart. If you're putting your name on something, use Claude.

**ChatGPT — Your Daily Assistant**
The most widely used for a reason. Great for daily tasks, quick drafts, customer FAQs, scheduling logic, brainstorming. The ecosystem of plugins and agents makes it incredibly powerful as an all-in-one workhorse. If you're just getting started with AI, this is your entry point.

**Gemini — Your Researcher**
Google's AI lives inside Gmail, Docs, and Sheets — which means zero learning curve if you're already in the Google ecosystem. Its real edge is handling massive amounts of information (2M+ token context) and synthesizing it fast. Use it when you're drowning in documents and need someone to make sense of it all.

**Perplexity — Your Scout**
Real-time web search with mandatory citations. This is your market intelligence tool. Competitive research, industry trends, current data — Perplexity gives you live answers with sources you can actually verify. I use it before every new client engagement to get current on their industry.
