# Wellness PE Operators — Fractional CMO / Commercial Architect Engagement Strategy
_Lynn Fernando · personal-brand consulting track (not REV Global) · drafted 2026-07-23_

## The core insight (why this audience, why now)
PE-backed wellness platforms are built by financial engineering, then need commercial engineering to hit the thesis. A sponsor buys 5–30 med spas, clinics, or studios, cleans up the EBITDA, and underwrites a value-creation plan that assumes organic growth. That organic growth is a marketing problem nobody on the deal team owns.

What the platform actually inherits: fragmented brands across locations, no unified demand-gen engine, rising patient/customer acquisition cost, weak retention and no CRM discipline, and no commercial playbook that scales past location eight. The operating partner needs someone to architect the commercial layer across the whole platform, not a marketing manager to run one clinic's Instagram.

That gap is exactly the Commercial Architect offer. And it is repeatable: land one platform, become the person the firm plugs into every wellness deal they close next.

## Positioning (how to frame it to this buyer)
- Lead as **Commercial Architect / commercial value creation**, not "fractional CMO." CMO reads as overhead; commercial architecture reads as multiple expansion. Keep "Fractional VP/CMO" as the engagement structure, never the headline.
- Speak their language: value-creation plan, 100-day plan, EBITDA, CAC, LTV, same-store growth, platform vs. add-on, exit readiness, multiple expansion.
- Lead proof with the two numbers that map to their world:
  - **6.8x exit multiple** for a $6M founder-owned business (consulting).
  - **$1.8M new ARR in 9 months** for a $4M B2B company.
  - Then the scale credibility: **$50M+** partnership value and **10:1 ROI** across four Disney properties (a multi-site commercial build that mirrors a roll-up), and Amazon Fashion CAC/conversion wins (**25%** conversion lift, **40%** engagement, **30%** repeat purchase).
- Anchor story to use: **Disney 50th** (multi-property, multi-location commercial strategy = the roll-up analog). Amazon for the DTC/customer-acquisition proof.

## Offer ladder (mapped to how PE buys)
1. **Commercial Diligence / 100-Day Teardown** — entry wedge. A fixed-scope commercial audit of one platform (or a live diligence target): brand architecture, demand-gen maturity, CAC/retention economics, cross-location scalability, and a prioritized value-creation roadmap. Priced as an enterprise engagement, closed via SOW, never quoted in a cold email.
2. **Fractional Commercial Lead / VP-CMO** — the land. $25K/mo structure, 3–4 days/week, embedded across the platform to build the commercial infrastructure the audit scoped.
3. **Firm-level retainer** — the expand and the real prize. Become the commercial architect the sponsor deploys into every new wellness platform. One relationship, repeatable across the fund's deal flow.

Do not pitch pricing in cold outreach. Close price via SOW after the fit call.

3. **Value email (Touch 1).** The roll-up commercial-gap thesis in two short paragraphs, one proof point (6.8x exit multiple or $1.8M ARR), CTA to a 15-minute call. Two-sentence paragraphs, no em dashes, contractions, humble and educational.
